# News and upcoming Events

- **2026-10-14: Release of a QoL-Update.** It fixes a lot of the bug-sources seen in recently failed experiments. Also, the performance of the observers was improved. There should be no harm in activating power-tracing and gpio-tracing at the same time anymore. Because of that the server now accepts experiments with custom samplerates in PowerTracing(). See newly added [benchmark-results for more](/content/infrastructure.md#benchmarks).

## Past Events

- our [extension of deployment](/content/deployment.md) is finished. A total of 24 nodes are now selectable for experiments. (March 2026)
- the capes of the current observers got replaced with v2.5, so the number of observable GPIO increased and the target can receive two power good signals, [see here for more info](/content/deployment.md#what-does-that-mean-for-the-user) (March 2026)
- the Launch of public access is in January 2026
- [Our Paper introducing Shepherd Nova](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3711875.3729146) got accepted and published at [MobiSys 2025](https://www.sigmobile.org/mobisys/2025/)
- In the second half of 2023 a semi-open instance of the Shepherd testbed went live.
